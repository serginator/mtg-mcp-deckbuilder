## Gameplan

The deck runs on drawing your second card each turn. **Bard the Bowman** rewards it with a +1/+1 counter and lifelink on any creature — stack those on **Ravenhill Flock**, which also grows by one on each draw and has flying, turning it into an aerial finisher. **Master's Councillors** turns each trigger into 3 cards milled from the opponent's library.

**Plunder the Trollshaws** and **Confusticate and Bebother** reliably fire the second-draw triggers. When Confusticate is used for "draw 2, discard 1" it triggers both Bard and Master's Councillors in one shot.

**Patient Instructor** and **Esgaroth Garrison** generate 1/1 Human Soldier tokens via Recruit. Esgaroth's power equals the number of creatures you control, so flooding the board with tokens turns it into a massive beater on turn 5-6. **Lake-town Toymaker** rewards having already drawn 2 by giving any creature +3/+0 and first strike before combat, turning even a 1/1 token into a combat threat.

**Eagle's Rescue** is the main equipment/aura: +2/+2 and flying for 4 mana, and it returns from the graveyard to any creature with power 1 or less — 1/1 recruit tokens are a free target. **Enchanted River's Grasp** locks down their best creature (taps it, strips abilities, prevents untap). **Magnificent End** deals 5 damage to a creature for just 2 mana if it's already tapped (which Grasp sets up).

---

## Key Synergies

- **Confusticate and Bebother → Bard the Bowman + Master's Councillors** — one spell fires both "second draw" triggers simultaneously
- **Patient Instructor / Esgaroth Garrison recruit → Esgaroth Garrison power** — each 1/1 token makes Garrison hit harder
- **Enchanted River's Grasp → Magnificent End** — tap + strip a creature, then Magnificent End costs {2}{W} to deal 5
- **Eagle's Rescue → 1/1 recruit tokens** — the recursion condition (power ≤ 1) fits tokens perfectly
- **Ravenhill Flock + any draw spell** — grows each time you draw; with Plunder the Trollshaws flashback it can grow 3 times in a single turn

## What to Cut First

- **Thorin's Last Stand** — only 1 copy; the first cut if you need space for extra interaction
- **Well-Worn Spatula** — low-impact equipment; replace with Dwarven Provisioner ×2 if you need more early bodies
- **Master's Councillors** — the mill is situational and the power boost rarely kicks in; cut if the meta doesn't fill graveyards fast
