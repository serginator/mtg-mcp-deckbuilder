# Dragon's Hoard

Gameplan: Build up Treasures early through Dori (makes one on ETB) and The Misty Mountains Cold (one per chapter, eventually spawns a 6/6 Dragon), then land Smaug the Magnificent and attack for massive direct damage scaled to your Treasure count. Smaug, Wicked Worm follows as the secondary threat — he creates more Treasures and draws cards whenever you spend Treasure mana. Smaug, the Great Calamity's adventure (Spew Flame) clears a big blocker for 5 mana before you eventually cast the 7-mana flying Dragon to close. Crude Bent Blade is the removal engine — ETB forces an opponent sacrifice then equips for +2/+1, no cost to your Treasures or board. Smaug's Fury is the combat trick to push through or win a key fight.
