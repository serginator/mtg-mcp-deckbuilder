# Duskmourn Sergio — Deck Guide

**Colors:** Black / Green with light Blue splash  
**Format:** Sealed (6 boosters)  
**Style:** Deathtouch beatdown with delirium grind

---

## Gameplan

Play efficient black creatures on curve, fill the graveyard to enable delirium, then close the game with threats that demand two blockers or trade up every time they fight. The deck has two speeds:

- **Aggressive:** Valgavoth's Faithful → Flesh Burrower / Balemurk Leech / Cynical Loner → Appendage Amalgam / Osseous Sticktwister. Apply pressure early and use Horrid Vigor to protect your best attacker.
- **Grindy:** Let creatures trade, fill the graveyard, unlock delirium. Resurrected Cultist comes back from the graveyard. The Swarmweaver turns your token Insects into deathtouch flyers. Osseous Sticktwister squeezes opponents every end step.

## Key Synergies

### 1. Delirium engine
**Goal:** Get 4+ card types in your graveyard (creature, instant, sorcery, enchantment, land, artifact all count).

- **Cynical Loner** — Tutors any card directly into the graveyard after attacking. Use it to find an instant or enchantment to complete delirium.
- **Commune with Evil** — Puts 3 cards into graveyard, gains 3 life, and puts 1 card in hand. Fills delirium fast.
- **Innocuous Rat** — When it dies it mills 2, putting whatever it finds into the graveyard.

Once delirium is active:
- **Osseous Sticktwister** — Each end step forces opponents to sacrifice a nonland permanent or discard. They will run out of answers.
- **Resurrected Cultist** — Pay {2}{B}{B} to return it from your graveyard to the battlefield.
- **The Swarmweaver** — Its two Insect tokens become 2/2 deathtouch flyers.

### 2. Deathtouch package
Every fight becomes a trade in your favor when deathtouch is involved.

- **Flesh Burrower** — On attack, gives another creature deathtouch until end of turn. Attack with Flesh Burrower + any big creature: opponent can't block safely.
- **Horrid Vigor** — Instant. Gives a creature deathtouch + indestructible. Use in combat to trade up, or to protect a key creature from removal.
- **Widow's Walk** (second door of Derelict Attic) — Whenever a creature attacks alone, it gets deathtouch. Pay {3}{B} to unlock after casting Derelict Attic.
- **Fear of the Dark** — Already has menace; if the opponent has no Glimmer creatures it also gains deathtouch. A 4/4 that needs 2+ blockers to survive contact is often unblockable in practice.

### 3. Balemurk Leech drain
Balemurk Leech ({1}{B}, 2/2) drains 1 life each time an **enchantment enters** or a **Room fully unlocks**. Cards that trigger it:

| Card | Triggers |
|---|---|
| Appendage Amalgam (×2) | 1 trigger each time it enters |
| Derelict Attic // Widow's Walk | 1 trigger on entry + 1 more when Widow's Walk unlocks |
| Derelict Attic alone | 1 trigger on entry |

In a game where you cast both Amalgams and unlock both Room doors, that is already 4–5 free damage.

### 4. Nashi's recovery
Nashi, Searcher in the Dark ({U}{B}, 2/2 menace) mills cards equal to damage dealt when it hits a player. It then lets you put **legendary and/or enchantment cards** from among them into your hand.

Cards it can recover:
- **The Swarmweaver** (legendary)
- **Fear of the Dark** (enchantment creature)
- **Appendage Amalgam** (enchantment creature)
- **Derelict Attic // Widow's Walk** (enchantment)

If Nashi hits and nothing was found, it gains a +1/+1 counter and will hit harder next time.

### 5. Silent Hallcreeper as finisher
Silent Hallcreeper ({1}{U}, 2/2) cannot be blocked. Each time it deals combat damage, choose one mode that hasn't been chosen yet:

1. +2/+2 counters permanently.
2. Draw a card.
3. Become a copy of another creature you control.

Mode 3 is the strongest late: copy The Swarmweaver (gets two flying Insect tokens), Fear of the Dark (4/4 with menace/deathtouch), or Osseous Sticktwister (second Sticktwister trigger every end step).

## Desired Opening Hand

A good 7-card hand has **all three** of these:

1. **3–4 lands**, including **at least 2 Swamps** (or Murky Sewer / Gloomlake Verge as substitutes). Most of the deck is black.
2. **A 2-drop** (Balemurk Leech, Flesh Burrower, Cynical Loner, Osseous Sticktwister, Nashi, or Silent Hallcreeper).
3. **Something to do on turn 3–4** (Appendage Amalgam, Commune with Evil, Spineseeker Centipede, Resurrected Cultist).

Removal in hand (Final Vengeance, Murder, Horrid Vigor) is a bonus, not a requirement. Do not keep a hand that is all removal with no creatures.

### Perfect opening hand example
```
Swamp · Swamp · Murky Sewer · Forest
Balemurk Leech · Appendage Amalgam · Horrid Vigor
```
- Turn 2: Leech.
- Turn 3: Amalgam (Leech triggers, drain 1).
- Turn 4: Hold up Horrid Vigor for combat.

## Mulligan Guide

### Always keep
- 3 lands + 2-drop + any action → keep 7.
- 2 lands + 2-drop + 3-drop + anything → keep (you will draw the third land).

### Always mulligan
- 0–1 lands.
- 5+ lands with only 1 spell.
- No black source (Swamp, Murky Sewer, Gloomlake Verge) with a hand full of black cards.
- 7 cards with only 4-mana+ spells and no action before turn 4.

### On 6 cards
Keep if you have: 2–3 lands, one 2-drop, and one other spell. Cut greedily — one redundant land or a late spell goes first.

### On 5 cards
Keep almost any hand with 2 lands and 2 spells. The deck has enough 2-drops that you will find action.

### Specific notes
- **Derelict Attic** in opening hand is great — it replaces itself when cast (draw 2, lose 2). Never mulligan because of it.
- **Valgavoth's Faithful** in opening hand is fine as a 1/1 body on turn 1; its reanimation ability is a late-game bonus.
- **Nashi** in opening hand requires a blue source by turn 2. If you have Nashi but no Swamp / Murky Sewer / Gloomlake Verge, the hand is risky on 7, dangerous on 6.
- **The Swarmweaver** in opening hand: keep it, but make sure you have enough early action to survive to turn 4.

## What to Cut First When Adapting

| Card | Cut when… |
|---|---|
| Valgavoth's Faithful | Opponent kills your best creatures before you reach 5 mana |
| Cynical Loner | Opponent has many Glimmers (it is blocked by them) or you need a bigger body |
| Lakeside Shack | You find a second Island or cut the blue cards entirely |
