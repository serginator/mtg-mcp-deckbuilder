## Gameplan

The deck wins by flooding the board with Frogs and triggering ETB loops. **Valley Mightcaller** is the primary threat: it gets a +1/+1 counter each time any Frog enters, and the bounce engine (Mistbreath Elder at upkeep, Stickytongue Sentinel on ETB, Run Away Together at instant speed) replays your Frogs repeatedly until it becomes an 8/8 trample by turn 5. **Stocking the Pantry** is the draw engine — it triggers whenever +1/+1 counters are placed, and **Sunshower Druid** places a counter on ETB, so every Frog that enters refuels your hand for free.

**Pond Prophet** draws a card on ETB, making every bounce also a cantrip. **Three Tree Scribe** rewards the bounce plan: whenever a creature leaves without dying it puts a counter on itself, growing into an additional threat over the course of the game. **Waterspout Warden** gains flying whenever another creature entered this turn — with 3+ Frogs entering per turn, you end up with an aerial clock the opponent cannot ignore. **Valley Floodcaller** (flash) pumps all Frogs and untaps them in response to any noncreature spell, enabling ambush blocks or an unexpected lethal alpha strike.

---

## Key Synergies

- **Sunshower Druid → Valley Mightcaller + Stocking the Pantry** — one ETB fires both: Mightcaller grows and Pantry draws a card
- **Longstalk Brawl with Gift → Stocking the Pantry** — the Gift puts a counter on your creature; Pantry triggers and draws you a card. For {G} you remove a threat, grow a Frog, and refuel.
- **Run Away Together → Three Tree Scribe + Stocking the Pantry** — bouncing your Pond Prophet triggers Scribe (creature leaves without dying → counter placed → Pantry draws). One {1}{U} instant = 2 draws and a free counter.
- **Mistbreath Elder → Three Tree Scribe** — upkeep bounce of any Frog triggers Scribe's counter; chain this every turn for free growth
- **Stickytongue Sentinel → Pond Prophet / Sunshower Druid** — bounce any ETB Frog back to hand on entering, then replay it for another draw and counter
- **Polliwallop + big Valley Mightcaller** — a 6/6 trample Mightcaller hits an opposing creature for 12 damage; effectively one-shots most threats
- **Dazzling Denial + wide board** — with 6 creatures in play it costs the opponent {6} to resolve anything, effectively a hard counter late-game
- **Long River Lurker + any Frog** — all Frogs gain ward {2} while Lurker is out, making targeted removal cost 2 extra mana per Frog

## What to Cut First

- **Clifftop Lookout** — only 1 copy; the ramp is good but the slowest payoff. First cut if you need a 4th Dazzling Denial or more bounce.
- **Valley Floodcaller** — only 1 copy; powerful but an Otter, not a Frog. Cut if you want to tighten the Frog count.
- **Long River Lurker** — 1-of; the ward effect is powerful but narrow. Cut if you need a 3rd Stickytongue Sentinel for more bounce consistency.

## Opening Hand & Mulligan

**Keep any hand that has:**
- 3–4 lands (2–3 if your curve tops at 2)
- At least 1 of: Sunshower Druid, Valley Mightcaller, Mistbreath Elder
- At least 1 of: Stocking the Pantry, Pond Prophet

The ideal opener is **Sunshower Druid + Stocking the Pantry + 3 lands** — engine online by turn 2: Druid enters → counter placed → Pantry draws a card.

**Mulligan if you have:**
- 0–1 lands or 6–7 lands
- No 1-drop creature — the deck lives and dies on turn 1 plays
- All interaction and no Frogs (Dazzling Denial, Polliwallop, Pawpatch — you need bodies to make these matter)
- Valley Mightcaller with no other Frogs in hand — it's a 1/1 trample without support

**The non-negotiable:** at least one Frog that costs 1. Sunshower Druid is best; Mistbreath Elder or Valley Mightcaller also work. Without a turn-1 Frog you fall behind on tempo and the ETB chain never gets going.
