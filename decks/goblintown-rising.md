# standard deck

## Gameplan

The Army token is the axis. Every Amass spell grows it and, with The Great Goblin in play, deals 2 damage per counter placed — Amass 2 equals 4 damage before combat. Land The Great Goblin by turn 3, then chain Goblin-town Flunkies, Tidings of War, Rage into the Valley, and the Clap! Snap! adventure ({1}{B}: Amass Goblins 2) to burn the opponent out while the Army grows. Stir Up Trouble at {B} (sac a Giant's Boulder or spent artifact) is the main cheap removal — sacrificing an artifact also unlocks Dreaded Bat-Cloud that same turn for just {1}{B}. Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern handles large creatures and artifacts nothing else can touch. Dori and The Misty Mountains Cold build Treasures to power Smaug the Magnificent's attack trigger for finishing reach.

## Key Synergies

**The Great Goblin + any Amass spell**
Every counter placed on a Goblin, Orc, or Army deals 2 damage to an opponent. Amass 1 = 2 damage, Amass 2 = 4 damage, Amass 3 = 6 damage — all before combat.

**Stir Up Trouble + Dreaded Bat-Cloud**
Sacrifice an artifact (Giant's Boulder, a spent Crude Bent Blade) to destroy a creature for {B}. A creature died this turn, so Dreaded Bat-Cloud now costs only {1}{B}. Two threats answered and deployed in the same turn for 2 mana total.

**Goblin-town Flunkies + The Great Goblin**
Flunkies has haste and Amasses 1 on ETB. Turn 3 with The Great Goblin already in play: play Flunkies, deal 2 damage on ETB, swing with haste. Instant 2 free damage every time you cast a Flunkies.

**Clap! Snap! (adventure) + Great Ugly-Looking Goblin**
Cast the adventure at {1}{B} for Amass Goblins 2 — that is 4 damage with The Great Goblin. Later cast the creature from exile: every creature you control with a +1/+1 counter (your Army and anything buffed) gains menace, making the board nearly unblockable.

**Goblin-town land + The Great Goblin**
Activate Goblin-town ({2}{B}{R}, sac it) to put 2 counters on the Army — triggers The Great Goblin for 4 damage. Use it when you are flooding on lands and need reach.

**Misty Mountains Raider + The Great Goblin**
Every attack trigger gives the Army 2 counters = 4 damage from The Great Goblin before blockers are assigned. Repeated every turn.

**Tidings of War + flashback**
Cast it from the graveyard for Amass Goblins 3 instead of 1. With The Great Goblin: 6 damage for {3}{R} from the graveyard.

**Gathering of Darkness**
Amass Goblins 3 (6 damage with The Great Goblin) AND returns a creature from your graveyard. Use it to recover The Great Goblin if he dies.

**Dori, Bearer of Friends + Smaug the Magnificent**
Each Dori ETB creates a Treasure. The Misty Mountains Cold adds more. Smaug deals damage equal to Treasures you control when he attacks — stockpile before swinging.

**Óin the Brave + Storied**
With 3+ artifacts, legendaries, or Sagas in play (easy with Giant's Boulder, Crude Bent Blade, The Great Goblin, Dori), Óin gains haste and +1/+0. He becomes a {1}{R} 2/1 haste that loots for free.

## Ideal Opening Hand

Keep any 7 that has:
- 2–3 lands (at least 1 Mountain and 1 Swamp, or Goblin-town)
- At least one 2-drop (Óin the Brave, Goblin-town Flunkies, Stony-Voiced Goblins, either Gollum)
- The Great Goblin OR a clear path to it by turn 3

The dream opener: 2 lands + Goblin-town Flunkies + The Great Goblin + any Amass spell. Turn 2 Flunkies (Amass 1, Army created), turn 3 The Great Goblin, turn 4 any Amass spell = 2+ damage directly to face on top of combat.

## Mulligan Guide

**Always mulligan:**
- 0–1 lands
- 5+ lands with no action
- No 2-drops AND no The Great Goblin AND no removal

**Borderline keep on 7, mulligan on 6:**
- 2 lands + The Great Goblin but no 2-drop (you miss turn 2 and fall behind)
- 4 lands + good spells (playable on 7, not on 6)

**Keep on 6:**
- 2 lands + The Great Goblin + any 2-drop
- 2 lands + two 2-drops + removal

**Keep on 5:**
- 2 lands + The Great Goblin + one other spell
- 2 lands + two cheap threats (Flunkies + Óin)

**Priority when scrying or looting:** always look for The Great Goblin first, then Amass spells. He is the engine — without him, Amass spells are just slow token creation.

## What to Cut First

- Misty Mountains Raider — slowest piece at 5 mana; cut if you need a faster curve
- Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern — powerful but slow; cut if the meta is very aggressive
