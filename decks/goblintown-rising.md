# standard deck

## Gameplan

The Army token is the axis. Every Amass spell grows it and, with The Great Goblin in play, deals 2 damage per counter placed — Amass 2 equals 4 damage before combat. Land The Great Goblin by turn 3, then chain Goblin-town Flunkies, Tidings of War, Rage into the Valley, and the Clap! Snap! adventure ({1}{B}: Amass Goblins 2) to burn the opponent out while the Army grows. Stir Up Trouble at {B} (sac a Giant's Boulder or spent artifact) is the main cheap removal — sacrificing an artifact also unlocks Dreaded Bat-Cloud that same turn for just {1}{B}. Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern handles large creatures and artifacts nothing else can touch. Dori and The Misty Mountains Cold build Treasures to power Smaug the Magnificent's attack trigger for finishing reach.

## Ideal Opening Hand

Keep any 7 that has:
- 2–3 lands (at least 1 Mountain and 1 Swamp, or Goblin-town)
- At least one 2-drop (Óin the Brave, Goblin-town Flunkies, Stony-Voiced Goblins, either Gollum)
- The Great Goblin OR a clear path to it by turn 3

The dream opener: 2 lands + Goblin-town Flunkies + The Great Goblin + any Amass spell. Turn 2 you play Flunkies (Amass 1, Army created), turn 3 you drop The Great Goblin, turn 4 any Amass spell deals 2+ damage directly to face on top of attacking.

## Mulligan Guide

**Always mulligan:**
- 0–1 lands
- 5+ lands with no action
- No 2-drops AND no The Great Goblin AND no removal

**Borderline keep on 7, mulligan on 6:**
- 2 lands + The Great Goblin but no 2-drop (you will miss turn 2 and fall behind on board)
- 4 lands + good spells (land-heavy but playable on 7, not on 6)

**Keep on 6:**
- 2 lands + The Great Goblin + any 2-drop
- 2 lands + two 2-drops + removal (you will find The Great Goblin)

**Keep on 5:**
- 2 lands + The Great Goblin + one other spell
- 2 lands + two cheap threats (Flunkies + Óin)

**Priority when scrying or looting (Óin, Giant's Boulder):** always look for The Great Goblin first, then Amass spells. He is the engine — without him, Amass spells are just slow token creation.

## What to Cut First

- Misty Mountains Raider — slowest piece at 5 mana; cut if you need a faster curve
- Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern — powerful but slow; cut if the meta is very aggressive
