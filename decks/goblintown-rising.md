## Gameplan

Flood the board with cheap Goblins in the first two turns, then pile counters onto the Army token through Amass (Flunkies, Goblin Plate Mail, Tidings of War). **The Great Goblin** converts every counter placed into 2 damage to any target — that's burn stapled to your creatures before combat even starts.

**Dori** and **The Misty Mountains Cold** build a Treasure stockpile that lets you drop **Smaug the Magnificent** a turn early for a flying hasty threat whose attack trigger scales with every Treasure you have left.

**Gollum, Riddle Master** is pure value: pick odd or even on ETB, then drain 2, draw, or grow whenever your opponent casts on that parity. At 2 mana it runs in the background all game.

**Crude Bent Blade** forces an opponent sacrifice, which immediately unlocks **Dreaded Bat-Cloud** at {1}{B} — a flying deathtoucher that stonewalls anything in the air.

---

## Key Synergies

- **Goblin-town Flunkies → The Great Goblin** — haste + Amass 1 becomes a 2-damage ping on the same turn
- **Crude Bent Blade → Dreaded Bat-Cloud** — sacrifice trigger discounts the Bat-Cloud to 2 mana
- **Dori / The Misty Mountains Cold → Smaug the Magnificent** — Treasures fuel early Smaug and scale his attack damage
- **Stony-Voiced Goblins** — drops alongside any Goblin or Army to strip a card on the way in

## What to Cut First

- **Front Porch Sentries** — the weakest body; replace with more removal or Smaug's Fury if pushing aggro
- **Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern** — powerful but slow (3-turn saga); cut for instant speed if the meta is fast
